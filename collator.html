Collator / a pgData builder for Rarebit

Rarebit keeps every page's title, date, alt text and author notes in one hand-written pgData array, and makes you bump maxpg by hand each time you post. Miss it and your newest page never shows. Drop your comic folder here and it writes both for you.

01 Point it at your pages

Drop your comics folder here or a pile of page files — pg1.jpg, pg2_1.jpg, pg2_2.jpg…

Filenames are read in your browser. No image ever leaves this page.

02 Match your Rarebit settings

These mirror the constants at the top of comic_settings.js. They only affect how filenames are parsed and what the output says.

03 Fill the dates in one go

Rarebit writes dates as writeDate(year, month, day). Use # in the title pattern for the page number — Chapter 1, # becomes Chapter 1, 4.
